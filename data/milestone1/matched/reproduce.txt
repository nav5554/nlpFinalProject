The launcher first reserves the evaluation sample, then collects sentence trajectories, fits the repository's original scorers, repeats MLP training at width 128, and evaluates all policies. A different hardware or software stack may produce different predictions from the recorded MPS run. Comparisons resample whole articles 10,000 times. Reference-containing-letter-or-digit metrics in audit.json use Python's Unicode isalnum on individual GPT-2 token decodes.
